# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Builders and curious owners exploring a proposed open offline information appliance. The showcase establishes the identity for a later on-device library, but is not itself that library. Visitors should understand the object, what knowledge it can hold, how they access it, and how it is built.

## Product Purpose

Omnipotent keeps useful knowledge locally accessible: medical journals, scientific papers, technical and service manuals, reference collections, books, audio/video, maps, and fabrication files. Explain browsing over local Wi-Fi versus taking compatible files onto a personal reader and letting the appliance sleep. Automatic updates and months of standby are intended goals, not measured capabilities.

## Brand Personality

Optimistic, resourceful, precise. Desirable field equipment rather than military survivalism. Physical material quality and understandable engineering should communicate confidence without inflated claims.

## Anti-references

The user rejected the generated mockups as tabloid-like, with oversized heavy typography, meaningless dials, and poor ornamental lines. Do not reuse their layout. Avoid generic SaaS cards, pretend telemetry, fake scientific sources, gratuitous instrument decorations, or an excessively captioned promotional presentation. Existing renders are product concepts, not manufacturing evidence.

## Design Principles

1. Show the object and its actual access patterns before specifications.
2. Precision means clear relationships and accurate information, not more decoration.
3. Use the enclosure and sled movement to explain modularity; do not invent separately animated parts.
4. Keep content open and provenance visible. Differentiate intent, illustration, and validated behavior.
5. Build the showcase with plain HTML5 and CSS, small purposeful vanilla JavaScript, no framework or build pipeline. A narrowly isolated Three.js renderer is approved for the incoming model.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, semantic structure, keyboard and touch access, readable typography, and reduced-motion behavior. Preserve the full story when JavaScript, WebGL, or motion is unavailable. Never require a 3D gesture or scroll animation to access the content.
